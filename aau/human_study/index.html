Face similarity study

Thank you for taking part. You will compare computer-generated faces. The images are renderings of synthetic 3D face models: no real person is shown, and no photograph of anyone is used.

What you will do

You will see 40 screens. Each shows one reference face at the top and two other faces below it. You pick the one that looks more similar to the reference. It takes about 10 minutes.

What is recorded

Only your choices and how long each one took. No name, no e-mail, no IP address, no cookie: the page runs entirely inside your browser and sends nothing anywhere. At the end you get a file and a participant code, and you decide whether to hand them over.

Voluntary participation

You can stop at any time by closing the tab, with no consequence and no partial data being kept. The anonymous answers will be used for academic research on 3D face similarity metrics and may be published in aggregate form.

Instructions

On every screen: the reference face is at the top, two candidate faces are below it, side by side.

Pick the candidate that looks more similar to the reference face.

  • Click on a face, or press 1 for the left one and 2 for the right one.
  • Go with your first impression. There is no right answer and no time limit, but about 10 seconds per screen is the right pace.
  • Judge the shape of the face: all faces are rendered with the same lighting, the same camera and no hair, skin tone or texture.
  • You cannot go back to a previous screen.

Some screens are easy on purpose: they are there to check that the page is being looked at.
