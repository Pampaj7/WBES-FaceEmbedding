\section{What Should a 3D Face Distance Measure?}
\label{sec:gt}

A meta-evaluation is only as good as its reference. The reference used in the
original version of this work normalizes every mesh by its own scale
(Sect.~\ref{sec:gt_maxabs}). Here we make the underlying question explicit,
answer it with the vocabulary of geometric morphometrics, and test the answer
on repeated real captures.

\paragraph{The thesis, made precise.}
``Alignment hurts'' conflates three operations.
(i) \emph{Pairwise transformations} (pairwise ICP, non-rigid fitting,
closest-point correspondence) are chosen anew for each pair; they break the
global consistency of the distance matrix and compress inter-subject
distances (Sect.~\ref{sec:distance_compression}). This part of the thesis
stands.
(ii) \emph{Per-identity scale normalization} removes facial size, which is an
identity trait: it is heritable~\cite{cole2016heritability}, it is useful
for 3D face recognition~\cite{russ2006normalization}, and on real captures it
separates people on its own (Sect.~\ref{sec:gt_arbiter}).
(iii) \emph{Per-identity rigid alignment} is necessary whenever head pose
cannot be observed from the geometry, as in every real scan. It redistributes
residuals locally (the Pinocchio effect~\cite{walker2000pinocchio,vonCramon2007pinocchio}),
which affects where differences are localized rather than the global
distance~\cite{klingenberg2020pinocchio}.
Only (i) and (ii) remove identity; (iii) belongs in a correct reference.

\subsection{Minimal Invariance: Form Versus Shape}
\label{sec:gt_principle}

Morphometrics calls \emph{shape} the geometry left after removing location,
rotation and scale~\cite{kendall1984shape,kendall1989survey}, and \emph{form}
(size-and-shape) the geometry left after removing location and rotation
only~\cite{dryden2016statistical,klingenberg2016size}. We adopt the following
principle.

\begin{quote}
\emph{Minimal invariance.} A reference distance is invariant only to the
transformations that the target data cannot observe. The transformation is
estimated once per identity, towards a fixed reference with a fixed rule,
never per pair, and with a resistant estimator~\cite{siegel1982resistant,rohlf1990extensions}.
\end{quote}

\noindent
For metric data (scans in millimetres, morphable models with known units)
only the rigid pose is unobservable, and the reference is a form distance.
For scale-free data, such as monocular reconstructions on NoW~\cite{now},
scale is unobservable too, and the reference is a shape distance. Current
benchmarks mix the two: NoW optionally fits scale~\cite{now}, REALY rescales
the ground truth per scan~\cite{REALY}, and MICA~\cite{mica} shows that
fitting scale lowers the NoW error of the mean FLAME template from 1.92 to
1.53\,mm and proposes a rigid-only protocol.
% fonte: literature/GT_FORM_SHAPE_2026-10-09.md ("MICA ... FLAME medio 1.92 -> 1.53 mm"; NoW e REALY nei Riferimenti)
We know of no 3D face reconstruction benchmark that separates form from shape.
% fonte: literature/GT_FORM_SHAPE_2026-10-09.md ("Nessun lavoro di valutazione ... separa form e shape")

\subsection{Reference Distances FR and SR}
\label{sec:gt_definition}

All domains are compared on 1{,}478 corresponding points of the FLAME face
region that every domain covers, reached through per-domain barycentric maps
(held-out landmark residual 0.61--1.28\,mm), with fixed area weights $W_v$
($\sum_v W_v=1$) computed on the global mean face $\mu$.
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e8/summary.md, Conclusioni 1-2 (1478 vertici; landmark tenuti fuori 0.61-1.28 mm); aau/runs/evidence/e12/protocol.md, Emendamento 1, E1 (pesi d'area fissi dalla media globale mu)
Each domain is converted to millimetres in the FLAME frame by one per-domain
rigid transformation; undeclared units (HIFI3D, FaceVerse) are calibrated to
an inter-pupillary distance of 63\,mm for the model mean, and the declared
ones are confirmed by the same measurement (60.8--67.9\,mm).
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/protocol.md, Emendamento 1, E1.1; aau/runs/evidence/e12/summary.md, Conclusioni 1
Let $f_i\in\mathbb{R}^{1478\times3}$ be the neutral configuration of identity
$i$. We fit one rigid motion per identity towards $\mu$ by iteratively
reweighted least squares with Tukey's biweight (cutoff $3\times$ the weighted
median residual, initialized at the least-squares fit),
\begin{equation}
a_i = R_i f_i + t_i,\qquad
(R_i,t_i)=\arg\min_{R,t}\sum_v W_v\,\rho_c\big(\|Rf_{i,v}+t-\mu_v\|\big),
\label{eq:robust_rigid}
\end{equation}
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/protocol.md, Emendamento 1, E1.1 ("Resistant fit: IRLS con perdita di Tukey ...")
and define the centroid $c_i=\sum_v W_v a_{i,v}$, the centroid size
$S_i=(\sum_v W_v\|a_{i,v}-c_i\|^2)^{1/2}$ in mm, and the unit-size pre-shape
$z_i=(a_i-c_i)/S_i$. The form reference \textbf{FR} and the shape reference
\textbf{SR} are
\begin{equation}
d_{\mathrm{FR}}(i,j)=\Big(\sum_v W_v\|a_{i,v}-a_{j,v}\|^2\Big)^{\frac12},\quad
d_{\mathrm{SR}}(i,j)=\Big(\sum_v W_v\|z_{i,v}-z_{j,v}\|^2\Big)^{\frac12}.
\label{eq:fr_sr}
\end{equation}
% fonte: v3_work/canonical_gt/train_fr_sr.py, docstring (definizioni di FR e SR)
Both are Euclidean distances between fixed per-identity vectors: no
transformation depends on the pair. Since $\sum_v W_v z_{i,v}=0$ and
$\sum_v W_v\|z_{i,v}\|^2=1$, they are linked exactly by
\begin{equation}
d_{\mathrm{FR}}^2=\|c_i-c_j\|^2+(S_i-S_j)^2+S_iS_j\,d_{\mathrm{SR}}^2,
\label{eq:size_shape}
\end{equation}
the size-and-shape decomposition $d_F^2=(S_i-S_j)^2+S_iS_j\,d_P^2$ of
Dryden and Mardia~\cite{dryden2016statistical} plus a centroid term, which is
non-zero because the resistant fit does not align centroids (median 1.1\% of
$d_{\mathrm{FR}}^2$ on HIFI3D). Eq.~\eqref{eq:size_shape} holds numerically
to a relative error below $7\times10^{-8}$.
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/train_gt.json, eval/hifi3d/identity (centroid_term_share_of_dFR2 median 0.0111; with_centroid_term_rel_err max 6.2e-08)
Dryden and Mardia use the optimal rotation per pair; we keep the
per-identity rotation to avoid pairwise transformations, at little cost (on
HIFI3D, median ratio 1.006 to the pairwise-optimal distance, Spearman 0.98).
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/train_gt.json, eval/hifi3d/identity (dP_common_rotation_over_pairwise_optimal median 1.0059; spearman_dP_common_vs_pairwise 0.9815)
The size term is a median 23\% of $d_{\mathrm{FR}}^2$ on HIFI3D and 28\% on
the FaMoS test subjects, but 1.6\% on FaceVerse and 6.9\% on FaceScape,
whose centroid-size variation (1.8\%, 1.6\%) is far below the 4.0--5.3\% of
FLAME, ICT, GNM, Multiface and FaMoS: these two models compress facial size.
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/train_gt.json, eval/*/identity/size_term_share_of_dFR2 (median hifi3d 0.234, famos_test 0.282, faceverse 0.016, facescape 0.069); aau/runs/evidence/e12/summary.md, sez. 2 (CV: faceverse 1.8%, facescape 1.6%; flame 4.3, ict 4.8, gnm 5.3, multiface 5.2, famos 4.0)
FR is the primary reference for metric data, SR for scale-free data. The
rigid fit is needed on morphable models too: on HIFI3D the face centroid moves
by a median 5.5\,mm (95th percentile 15.9\,mm) between identities in the
generator frame, and without the fit every evaluated method scores a Spearman
correlation of at most 0.119, because the reference mostly measures where the
generator places the face.
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/summary.md, Conclusioni 3 e 5 (spostamento 5.5 mm, p95 15.9; "Con F pura tutti i metodi stanno fra 0.005 e 0.119")

repeated captures. We use the FLAME registrations of all 95 FaMoS people

\subsection{Why Not the Legacy Maxabs Reference}
\label{sec:gt_maxabs}

The legacy reference centres each mesh on its vertex mean, divides it by its
own maximum absolute coordinate, and averages per-vertex Euclidean distances.
% fonte: aau/zs3dmm/build_zs_gt.py (normalize_maxabs + vertex_mean_l2_matrix); v2_work/genflame/build_flame_gt_matrix.py:28-30
\todo{allineare con Eq.~\eqref{eq:ground_truth_distance} del draft principale, che scrive un RMS e non una media di norme}
It has three problems. \emph{Its scale divisor depends on the support}: the
maximum coordinate belongs to one boundary vertex, the vertical half-extent
of the patch for 492 of 500 HIFI3D identities, so it moves with the crop line
on forehead or chin.
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/protocol.md, sez. 0 ("l'estensione verticale in 492 casi su 500 di HIFI3D"); aau/multiface/ws3a_render.py:101-103
\emph{It removes size}, the trait the arbiter finds informative; on HIFI3D
its Spearman correlation with FR is 0.391 [0.29, 0.49].
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/summary.md, sez. 3, hifi3d (riga "F + rigida robusta", colonna maxabs)
\emph{It favours models that share its divisor}: our earlier encoders
normalize their input the same way.
% fonte: paper/PLAN_MASSIVE.md, sez. 16.1 ("Il modello e108 normalizza l'ingresso con lo stesso divisore della maxabs")
Re-evaluating 22 methods on HIFI3D with identical distances and bootstrap
replicates, changing only the reference, reverses the ranking
(Table~\ref{tab:gt_reeval}). Under maxabs the encoders lead and beat Chamfer
by $+0.258$ [$+0.208$, $+0.301$]; under FR pairwise NICP leads, beating our
earlier encoder by $+0.174$ [$+0.092$, $+0.253$], and the encoder's margin over
Chamfer is no longer significant ($+0.039$ [$-0.021$, $+0.098$]). A size-blind
encoder cannot compete under a form reference; this motivates the
size-and-shape model of Sect.~\ref{sec:method} \todo{rimando alla sezione del modello fattorizzato}.
% fonte: aau/runs/evidence/e12/summary.md, Conclusioni 5 e sez. 5, HIFI3D nocrop_cross (delta appaiati); 22 righe = 15 in tabella + 7 solo in aau/runs/evidence/e12/methods_spearman.csv
We keep maxabs only as a legacy column.
