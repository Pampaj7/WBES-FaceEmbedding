Dear FaceScape Team,

I would like to request access to the FaceScape dataset for academic research at the Technical
University of Denmark (DTU).

**Who we are.** NOME SUPERVISORE leads the group; I am Leonardo Pampaloni, a PhD researcher.
Our work concerns the evaluation of 3D face reconstruction — specifically, how well different
distance measures preserve the ordering of facial identities when the meshes being compared
differ in resolution, connectivity or surface extent.

**What we would use FaceScape for.** Our current benchmark is built entirely from synthetic
identities sampled from a morphable model, and it varies topology while holding expression
fixed. FaceScape would let us test the axis we cannot currently touch: with 20 expressions per
subject in a uniform topology, it separates *identity* from *facial configuration*, which is
precisely the distinction a distance measure claiming to rank identities must make and which we
have so far been unable to evaluate at all. We are interested primarily in the TU models; the
multi-view images and camera parameters would additionally let us reconstruct the same faces
through a different pipeline, giving genuinely distinct topologies of one identity.

**Intended use and compliance.** Non-commercial academic research only. We will not
redistribute the data or any derivative from which the original models could be reconstructed,
and access will be limited to named members of our group. We understand that publication of
subject imagery is restricted to the approved publishable list, and that textures for subjects
360–847 are masked; we will respect both, and will confine any figures to subjects on the
publishable list. We will cite the FaceScape papers (CVPR 2020 and TPAMI 2023) in any resulting
publication.

Please let me know the current license form and the steps you would like us to follow. We are
happy to have the agreement countersigned by our institution if that is required.

With thanks for making this resource available,
