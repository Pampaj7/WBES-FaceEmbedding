Dear Professor Yin,

I am writing to request access to the BU-3DFE database for academic research at the Technical
University of Denmark (DTU), Department of DIPARTIMENTO.

**Research context.** Our group studies the evaluation of 3D face reconstruction. Rather than
measuring per-pair surface error, we are concerned with whether a distance measure preserves the
correct ordering of facial identities when the compared meshes differ in resolution,
connectivity or surface support — a property that current benchmarks do not test directly.

**Why BU-3DFE.** Our present benchmark is synthetic and varies only mesh topology; it holds
expression fixed, which means we cannot currently distinguish a measure that tracks identity
from one that merely tracks facial shape. BU-3DFE is well suited to that question because it
provides 25 models per subject spanning six expressions at four graded intensities, which gives
a controlled range of within-identity variation rather than a binary neutral/expressive split.
The graded intensities are the specific feature we need: they let us ask at what point expressive
deformation begins to dominate identity in a given distance measure.

**Intended use.** Non-commercial academic research only. We will not redistribute the database
or derivatives from which the original models could be reconstructed, will not use it for
commercial purposes or for subject identification beyond the provided labels, and will restrict
access to researchers named in the agreement. We will cite the BU-3DFE papers in any resulting
publication and will follow any additional conditions you specify.
